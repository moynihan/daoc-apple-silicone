Dark Age of Camelot on your Mac (Apple Silicon)
================================================

1. Double-click "Install DAoC.command".

   If macOS says it can't be opened or "cannot verify" it:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
   "Open Anyway", then double-click the file again.

   (Alternative with no security dialogs: open Terminal, paste this
   line, press Return:
   curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/moynihan/daoc-apple-silicone/main/install.sh | zsh )

2. A Terminal window shows progress. It may ask for your Mac password
   once, to install Apple's Rosetta.

3. The DAoC patcher appears and downloads the game (about 4.5 GB).
   When the top-left corner says 100%, press Play, then log in with
   your DAoC account. (Free "Endless Conquest" accounts:
   https://accounts.eamythic.com/ )

   If the game ever closes itself right after you press Play, just
   open it again and log in.

4. From then on, open "Dark Age of Camelot" from your Applications
   folder (the one inside your home folder: Finder > Go > Home > Applications).

Everything is installed inside ~/Applications/Dark Age of Camelot/.
To uninstall, drag that folder and the "Dark Age of Camelot" app to the Trash.
If something goes wrong, the log file is:
   ~/Applications/Dark Age of Camelot/wine.log
